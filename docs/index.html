Pulse In-Center Operations
Donor Journey

Every visit, one smooth loop.

From the first text to the thank-you — here's what the donor experience looks like with a phone, a wristband, or both. Zero friction, full visibility.

Before the visit

A branded text, not an app download

The donor gets a text with a link — tap to confirm, reschedule, or let you know they're running late. No app, no login, no friction. Works on any phone.

"Oh nice, I can just tap — I don't have to call or download anything."
Arrival

Walk in and you're checked in

Donors with a phone scan a poster QR and confirm — they're in the queue instantly. Donors without a phone (or who'd rather not use one) get a wristband after screening that tracks their journey the same way. Either path, same experience. Walk-ins register in seconds.

"I left my phone in the car — they just handed me a wristband and I was good to go."
In the queue

An honest wait, not a guessing game

Donors with their phone see a live, self-updating wait range — realistic, not promised. If the estimate can't be confident, it gracefully falls back to a position count. Wristband-only donors stay visible in the queue and get called forward by staff the same way. Either path, nobody's forgotten.

"I know roughly how long, so I can grab a coffee and come back."
Called forward

One notification when it's actually your turn

Pulse only sends this when the donor is cleared and a spot is genuinely ready — high confidence, no false calls. The donor walks up to something, not to a second wait.

"They texted me exactly when to come up. No name shouted across the room."
In the chair

A calm moment, not a clinical stare

During the donation, the donor's phone becomes a quiet companion — optional, one-handed, supportive. Not entertainment, not medical. A gentle primer for what's next, including a preview of their recovery perk.

"This is actually... kind of pleasant? And there's a perk waiting for me?"
Recovery

One tap — bed freed, timer started, perk unlocked

At the snack area, the donor scans their wristband or taps on their phone. That single gesture frees their donation spot, starts the center's observation timer, and reveals a sponsor perk and a quick satisfaction survey. Phone or band — same tap, same result.

"Free coffee from the place next door? And all I did was scan my wristband."
After departure

The visit ends, the care doesn't

Thank-you, milestone recognition, and an easy path to rebook. Then, wellness check-ins go out by text — a simple "how are you feeling?" If anything's off, your team gets a callback prompt. Every touchpoint is documented.

"They actually checked in on me the next day. That's... really thoughtful."
One loop. Every donor. Documented.
