The Pulse of Every Donation

Queue management, donor experience, and recovery assurance

for community blood centers.

The Problem

Full visibility, from check-in to check-out.

⏳

Blind Before & After

Centers have strong visibility during donation — almost none into what happens in the lobby or after the donor leaves.

📋

No Recovery Documentation

Post-donation adverse events (vasovagal reactions) are the #1 source of donor complaints and liability — and most go untracked.

🚪

Walkouts & No-Shows

Donors wait without information, leave without telling anyone, and don't come back. Centers have no real-time flow tools.

🔧

Legacy Systems

Existing donor management systems handle clinical records well but don't touch operational flow, experience, or post-visit care.

The Solution

The operational loop of a blood drive, run automatically.

Pulse runs check-in, screening, donation, recovery, and follow-up — giving centers real-time floor visibility while building a documented chain of care for every donor, automatically.

Web-Only, Text-Driven

Real-Time Queue Visibility

Donors interact through a branded text link. No app download, no hardware burden. Honest wait estimates, live pipeline status, and automated donor notifications.

Recovery Assurance

Operational Intelligence

Tracked observation windows, wellness follow-ups, and exportable duty-of-care records. Signal-driven state inference — staff run the floor, the system captures what's happening.

Recovery Assurance

A risk & compliance product with donor experience baked in.

The industry's most common adverse event — vasovagal reactions — often presents after the donor leaves. Centers usually learn about it only when the donor calls to complain. Pulse closes that gap as a byproduct of running the drive.
